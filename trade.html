FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Who is the PHYLUM Trade Program for?

Interior designers, architects, stylists, and stagers. If you specify or style with home goods for clients, you can apply.

What is the trade offer?

Approved members receive 15% off plus Joy loyalty points. Both apply on qualifying purchases. That is the double-dip: the discount does not replace the points.

What are Joy loyalty points?

Joy loyalty points are the points in PHYLUM’s loyalty program, presented to customers as PHYLUM Rewards. Trade members earn them in addition to the 15% discount. Current earn and redeem terms are on the PHYLUM Rewards page.

How do I apply?

Use the form on this page. Share your name, business, email, phone, website or portfolio, and trade type. A resale or tax ID is optional.

What happens after I apply?

PHYLUM reviews the application and follows up by email about next steps. Approval is not automatic, and this page does not promise a specific turnaround time.

Where is PHYLUM?

The flagship is in the Palm Springs Design District at 901 N. Palm Canyon Drive, Suite 101, Palm Springs, CA 92262. An outpost is inside THE PLACE in Indio. Many of the same makers are also online at shopphylum.com.

Does the 15% apply to everything?

The published offer is 15% off for approved trade members. Exclusions, including sale items, gift cards, or special orders, are not listed on this page and will be confirmed with your account.

Do I need a resale certificate?

The form asks for a resale or tax ID if you have one. It is optional. If a resale certificate is required for your account, PHYLUM will ask for it during review.
