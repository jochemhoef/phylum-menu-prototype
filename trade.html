FAQ

Straight answers

Who is the PHYLUM Trade Program for?

Interior designers, architects, stylists, and stagers. If you specify or style with home goods for clients, you’re our people.

What is the trade offer?

Approved members get 15% off plus PHYLUM Rewards points, both on qualifying purchases. That’s the double-dip: the discount doesn’t replace the points.

What is PHYLUM Rewards?

PHYLUM Rewards is PHYLUM’s loyalty program. Trade members earn PHYLUM Rewards points in addition to the 15% discount. Current earn and redeem terms are on the PHYLUM Rewards page.

How do I apply?

Use the form on this page. Share your name, business, email, phone, website or portfolio, trade type, and your interior design license or accreditation. A resale certificate is optional.

What happens after I apply?

A real person at PHYLUM reviews it and follows up by email with next steps. Approval isn’t automatic, and we’d rather not promise a turnaround we can’t keep.

Where is PHYLUM?

The flagship is in the Palm Springs Design District at 901 N. Palm Canyon Drive, Suite 101, Palm Springs, CA 92262. An outpost is inside THE PLACE in Indio. Many of the same makers are also online at shopphylum.com.

Does the 15% apply to everything?

Almost. Approved trade members get 15% off, and any exclusions (sale items, gift cards, or special orders, for example) are confirmed with your account.

Do I need a resale certificate?

Only for tax-exempt purchases. It’s optional on the application; add it if you plan to buy for resale without paying sales tax.
