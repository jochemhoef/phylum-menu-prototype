Absolutely. A virtual consultation brings the same guidance to wherever you are. We’ll walk the shop floor for you.

A few of our favorites are prepared especially for you by the studios we carry. It’s a mark of care, and worth the wait. We’ll flag these as we build your list together.

Never. Shipping is always complimentary on registry gifts, and so is the gift wrap. Every gift arrives ready for the occasion.

Registry gifts follow our standard return and exchange policy, the same warm, sensible one as everything else in the shop.
