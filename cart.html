    For the PHYLUM team · Black Friday Weekend

    Behind the cart: how the weekend's credit gets issued

    The campaign runs Wed, Nov 25 to Mon, Nov 30. Shopify Flow tags every qualifying order as it comes in, online and in store. On Tuesday, Dec 1 the team works through one list in Shopify Orders, or one scheduled Flow issues every credit at once.

    01The ladder

    One credit per transaction, on the amount paid. The credit is added on Tuesday, Dec 1 and never expires.

    02Tuesday, Dec 1: the "Store credit due" list

    A saved view in Shopify Orders. Flow has tagged every qualifying weekend order, so on Dec 1 the list is ready to work through. Try issuing one.

    Orders›Store credit due tag:credit-due
    OrderCustomerPaidTierCreditTagsStatus

    Simplified mock of the Shopify Orders list. Order numbers and customers are made up.

    03Set up once

    Everything below is built into Shopify. There's no app subscription and no customer data leaves the store.

    ATurn on the slide-in cart

    Palo Alto, your current theme, ships with a cart drawer. It's a single setting:

    Theme settings › Cart › Cart type › Drawer

    The full cart page keeps working at /cart, and the drawer links to it.

    Theme · 1 minute

    BAdd the Black Friday credit bar

    One snippet, phylum-credit-ladder.liquid, is rendered in both the drawer and the cart page. Tiers, dates and copy live in theme settings. The bar switches itself on for Nov 25 and off after Nov 30, so nobody has to remember.

    Theme · we build it

    CA Flow workflow tags qualifying orders

    A second small workflow watches refunds. If a return drops an order below its tier before Dec 1, it swaps credit-due for credit-review.

    Shopify Flow · free, included

    DOn Dec 1, issue from one saved view

    1. In Orders, filter Tagged with: credit-due and save the view as Store credit due.
    2. Open an order › customer › Store credit › Issue credit. Enter the amount from the tag, leave the expiry empty (it never expires), and tick Notify customer.
    3. Replace the tag credit-due with credit-issued, and the order drops off the list.
    Team · ~20 seconds per order

    04Or: one scheduled run on Dec 1

    Instead of clicking through the list, Flow can issue every credit at 9am on Dec 1 by itself.

    FlowScheduled, then hands-off

    Scheduled time trigger (Tue, Dec 1, 9am) › Get order data for tag:credit-due › For each order › Send Admin API request with Shopify's native store credit mutation:

    # Flow › Send Admin API request › storeCreditAccountCredit
{
  "id": "{{ order.customer.id }}",
  "creditInput": {
    "creditAmount": { "amount": "{{ 15 | 30 | 60 | 150 from the tag }}", "currencyCode": "USD" },
    "notify": true
  }
}
# no expiresAt, so the credit never expires

    Then swap the tag to credit-issued. Shopify's own Store credit issued email goes out (edit it in Settings › Notifications). Test it on one staff order first.

    AppOr a turnkey app

    Apps like Rise.ai and Koin run spend-more campaigns on top of Shopify's native store credit, with their own reporting. For a single weekend they're more than PHYLUM needs.

    Either way, the credit is native Shopify store credit. Customers see their balance in their account, and it applies at checkout and at the register as a payment method.

    05The rules

    Confirmed in the campaign

    One credit per transaction

    The highest tier only. A $1,050 order gets $150, not $15 + $30 + $60 + $150.

    On the amount paid

    Flow reads what the customer actually paid, after discounts.

    Added Tuesday, Dec 1

    Every weekend credit lands on the same day, which keeps the team's work to one sitting.

    Never expires

    Leave the expiry date empty when issuing. Customers can spend it whenever they like.

    In store and online

    POS sales trigger the same Flow, and the register can redeem store credit, so it works on Palm Canyon Drive too.

    Free shipping all weekend

    Online orders ship free Nov 25–30, whatever their size. That's a shipping discount, separate from the credit.

    Still to decide before Nov 25

    Returns before Dec 1

    Should a return that drops an order below its tier lower the credit? The refund workflow flags these as credit-review either way.

    Tax and gift cards

    Does "amount paid" include tax, and do gift card purchases count? We'd suggest no to both.

    A customer on every POS sale

    Credit needs a customer with an email, so staff should attach one at the register for every qualifying sale.
