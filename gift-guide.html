When do I need to order for delivery by Christmas Eve?

For Standard Shipping, order by 3pm PT on Friday, December 11. From December 12, choose Priority Shipping at checkout and order by 3pm PT on Tuesday, December 15. These dates apply within the contiguous U.S. Past those dates, call or text us at (760) 424-2110 for a rush shipping quote and we’ll do everything we can.

How long before my order ships?

Please allow 1 to 2 business days for us to wrap and pack your gifts properly. Orders placed after 3pm PT, on weekends, or on federal holidays begin processing the next business day. You’ll receive tracking by email the moment your order ships.

What does shipping cost?
Orders of $150 or more
Standard Shipping is free within the contiguous U.S. and arrives 2 to 5 business days after it ships. Priority Shipping is an additional $35.
Orders under $150
Standard Shipping is $12 within the contiguous U.S. and arrives 2 to 5 business days after it ships. Priority Shipping is an additional $45.
Alaska, Hawaii, or Canada
Call or email hello@shopphylum.com for a custom quote.
Do you deliver locally?

Yes, and it’s on us. We offer free delivery to Palm Springs, Cathedral City, Rancho Mirage, Palm Desert, and Indian Wells. Local delivery depends on our team’s availability and arrives within 1 to 3 days of your order. Need it sooner? Call or text (760) 424-2110 to request urgent delivery and we’ll do everything we can.

Can you help me choose the right gift?

Absolutely. It’s our favorite part of the job. Tell us who you’re shopping for, what they love, and what you’d like to spend, and we’ll put together a few ideas. Call or text (760) 424-2110, or stop by the flagship, open daily from 10am to 5pm.

Is gift wrap really free?

Every order, online or in store, is wrapped by our team at no charge. We take it as seriously as the gift itself.

Can I include a gift note?

Of course. On any product page, check “Add gift wrap (always complimentary)” and an “Optional gift message” box appears. You can also add one in your cart under “Is this a gift? Add a note.” We’ll write it by hand on a note card, the old-fashioned way.

What if it isn’t quite right?

Then let’s find what is. The most graceful fix is an exchange or store credit in the recipient’s name for the full value, so the gift stays a gift. A refund can only go back to the card used at purchase, which means it lands with the giver.

Holiday gifts get extra time: anything purchased through Christmas Day can be exchanged through January 31, 2027. All other returns and exchanges are accepted within 30 days of purchase. Items and packaging must be in perfect, original condition. Anything used or worn will be politely declined. A receipt is required, and return shipping is the customer’s responsibility. Special order and sale items are final sale.

What if my package arrives damaged?

Contact us within 48 hours of delivery and we’ll make it right.
