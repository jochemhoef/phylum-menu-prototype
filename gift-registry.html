FAQ

Ask away. We love a group chat.

PHYLUM Registry DeskPalm Springs · replies in a flash

Hi! Ask us anything. Well, these eight things. Tap a question.

Wait, is this only for weddings?

Not even a little. Weddings, housewarmings, the holidays, a new baby, a big birthday: any time people keep asking what to bring. Want a list without guest tracking? That’s what the wishlist is for.

How do I start one?

Tap Start a registry and sign in with a PHYLUM account, then add the pieces you love as you shop. Rather do it in person? The Palm Springs flagship can start the list with you.

How do my guests find it?

They search by your first or last name on Find a registry. No account needed to look or to buy, and the list shows what’s still needed.

Registry vs. wishlist. Explain like I’m five.

A registry is made to share: guests can see what’s been bought and what’s still needed. A wishlist is a private list just for you, no guests, no tracking. You cross things off yourself.

Do the gifts ship?

They check out like any PHYLUM order, so they can ship or be picked up in Palm Springs. The shop offers complimentary shipping on orders over $150. Check the offer at checkout before you count on it.

Can I register in the store?

Yes, and we love it when you do. We’re at 901 N. Palm Canyon Drive, Suite 101, in the Palm Springs Design District. Call (760) 424-2110 if you’d like someone ready to build the list with you.

What if something sells out?

Good things go fast, so the list can’t promise every piece stays in stock. Guests always see what’s still needed, and there’s plenty more to love in the shop.

Okay but what does it cost?

Nothing. The registry, the wishlist, and the PHYLUM account you need are all free. Already have a list? Find it in your account.
