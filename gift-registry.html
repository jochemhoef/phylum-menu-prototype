FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is the PHYLUM registry only for weddings?

No. Use it for a wedding, the holidays, a housewarming, or any occasion where people have asked what to bring. A wishlist is there when you want a list without guest tracking.

How do I create a registry?

Choose Create a registry and sign in with a PHYLUM account. You can also start one in person at the Palm Springs flagship.

How do guests find a registry?

Guests search by first or last name on Find a registry. They don’t need an account to view the list or to buy from it.

What’s the difference between a registry and a wishlist?

A registry is meant to be shared. It can show what has been bought and what is still needed. A wishlist is a simpler private list, with no guest tracking. You remove things yourself once you have them.

Do registry gifts ship?

They check out like any other PHYLUM order. The shop publishes complimentary shipping on orders over $150, and Palm Springs pickup is available. Confirm the shipping offer on the order before you rely on it.

Can I register in the store?

Yes. The flagship is at 901 N. Palm Canyon Drive, Suite 101, in the Palm Springs Design District. Call (760) 424-2110 if you’d like someone there to start the list with you.

What if something on the list sells out?

Guests can see what’s still needed. If a piece is gone, they can choose something else from the edit. The list doesn’t promise that every item stays in stock.

Is there a fee?

Creating a PHYLUM registry or wishlist is free. You need a PHYLUM account, which is also free. Return to a list you’ve already made from your account.
