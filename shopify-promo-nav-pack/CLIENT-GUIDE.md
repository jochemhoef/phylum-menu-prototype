# Editing the PHYLUM mega menu

You can change the mega menu from the **theme customizer** without editing code.

## Open the editor

1. **Online Store → Themes**
2. On your theme, click **Customize**
3. Click the header / **PHYLUM mega menu** section in the left sidebar

## Logo and icons

- **Logo** — upload or replace the wordmark
- Toggle **Search** and **Account** icons on or off
- **Menswear dropdown mode** — *Promo* shows the green “coming soon” panel; *Catalog* shows product columns (if configured)

## Change a dropdown (example: Accessories)

1. Expand the **Accessories** block (or add a **Menu item** block)
2. Set **Column headings** (Wear, Adorn, Create, etc.)
3. For each column, choose a **Column menu** — lists come from **Online Store → Navigation**

### Update links in a column

1. **Online Store → Navigation**
2. Open the menu used for that column (e.g. “Mega - Accessories - Create”)
3. Add, remove, or reorder links — changes appear in the mega menu automatically

## Brand spotlight (right-hand image)

In each menu item block:

- **Show brand spotlight card**
- **Spotlight image**, **Eyebrow**, **Title**, **Link label**, **Spotlight link**

## Menswear / Coming soon

Use the block where **This is the Menswear item** is enabled:

- **Coming Soon** pill text
- **Promo** image, eyebrow, title, body text
- **Waitlist** — paste your Klaviyo/app **embed HTML**, or configure the default email form action with your developer

## Reorder top-level menu labels

In the customizer, drag **Menu item** blocks up or down.

## Need a new top-level category?

Click **Add block → Menu item**, set the title, columns, and spotlight like the others.
