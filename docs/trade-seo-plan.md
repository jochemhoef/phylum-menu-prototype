# PHYLUM Trade Program — SEO plan

Goal: rank the signup page for designers and architects who are looking for a to-the-trade source of home goods, and turn that traffic into applications.

The page this plan describes is [trade.html](../trade.html), published at `https://phylum-menu.onrender.com/trade.html`. PHYLUM’s shop lives at [shopphylum.com](https://shopphylum.com). When the same page moves onto Shopify, change the canonical URL to the shop domain and 301 the Render URL. Until that page exists, the canonical stays on this URL so we do not point search engines at a 404.

## Audience and location

PHYLUM is a modernist mercantile. The flagship is in the Palm Springs Design District at 901 N. Palm Canyon Dr., Suite 101, Palm Springs, CA 92262. There is an outpost inside THE PLACE in Indio. The trade audience is interior designers, architects, stylists, and stagers who specify tabletop, textiles, and objects — locally in the Coachella Valley and remotely for projects.

Public loyalty program: [PHYLUM Rewards](https://shopphylum.com/pages/phylumpoints). The trade offer stacks a 15% discount on top of Joy loyalty points (the double-dip). Do not publish earn rates, exclusions, or approval times on the landing page until the client confirms them.

Do not stuff the H1; the positioning line (“your favorite designer’s favorite store”) stays the headline, and the keyword-bearing title tag does the ranking work.

## Content ideas

Ship these as short pages or journal entries that link to the signup form. Each one should answer a search, not restate the offer.

1. **A trade day in the Design District.** What a designer can pull from the flagship in one visit: tabletop, textiles, objects, gift wrap. Link to the application and to the brand directory.
2. **Specifying for desert houses.** How the edit (Hasami, Chilewich, Ekobo, Studio Arhoj, David Mellor, and the rest of the directory) works in Palm Springs and Indio projects. Local query bait without fake project claims.
3. **Trade pricing and loyalty, explained.** The double-dip in plain language, once exclusions and the Joy earn rate on discounted orders are confirmed. Until then, keep this off the site.
4. **For stylists and stagers.** A tighter page than the main signup, aimed at short-term pulls rather than ongoing specification.
5. **Brand notes for specifiers.** One maker at a time, using copy and photos already on the brand directory, each ending with a trade CTA.
6. **FAQ expansion.** Add questions only after the client answers them: minimums, net terms, whether the discount is in store and online, resale-certificate rules, and how an approved account actually checks out.

## Internal linking

On this prototype:

- Home (`index.html`) links to the trade page from the primary nav and from a hero button. The mobile menu includes the same link.
- The trade page links back to home, to `brands.html`, and to the live shop, about, and rewards pages.
- The brand directory is the natural second click after someone applies.

When this moves to Shopify:

- Add “Trade” to the footer and to the header, not only a homepage banner.

- Give each major collection a line of copy and a text link to the trade page where the audience is designers, not a sitewide popup.
- Add the final Shopify URL to the sitemap and to Search Console.

## Local search

- Keep the name, address, and phone on the page identical to the [Google Business Profile](https://maps.app.goo.gl/4PkCwBXmNPtHUFLD7) and to shopphylum.com.
- Mention Palm Springs Design District, Indio, and the Coachella Valley in body copy, which this page does.
- Ask approved trade partners, when the relationship is real, for a Google review that mentions specifying or trade — do not solicit fake reviews.
- Add the trade URL to the Business Profile website or a product/post once the page lives on shopphylum.com. The Render prototype should not replace the shop as the profile’s website.

## Backlinks and outreach

Aim for links from pages designers already trust. A link is useful when the surrounding page is about sourcing, Palm Springs design, or the trade.

- **Local design press and guides:** Palm Springs Life, DESERT magazine, local Design District roundups, Modernism Week partner or exhibitor pages if PHYLUM participates.
- **Professional groups:** Palm Springs and Coachella Valley ASID and AIA chapter resource lists, IIDA, and staging associations. Offer a factual one-paragraph listing, not a discount code posted in public.
- **Makers already in the shop:** Chilewich, Hasami, Ekobo, Studio Arhoj, David Mellor, and the rest of the directory. Ask to be listed as a Palm Springs stockist, with the trade page as the “for designers” link.
- **Designers who already shop PHYLUM:** a short project credit (“sources: PHYLUM, Palm Springs”) when they are willing. Do not write the credit for them.
- **Hospitality and real-estate stylists** who publish house features in the valley. One sourced room is worth more than a directory blast.
- **Complementary, non-competing businesses** on Palm Canyon (studios, showrooms, installers) for a mutual “resources” mention.

Skip paid link networks, footer swaps, and exact-match anchor text. The anchor should read naturally: “PHYLUM Trade Program” or “to-the-trade source in Palm Springs.”

## Measurement

- Search Console on the final domain: impressions and clicks for the primary keywords, and the queries that include “Palm Springs,” “trade,” “designer,” and “discount.”
- Application starts and completed submits, once the form posts somewhere real. The current form does not submit.
- Assisted conversions: trade applications that later shop, once accounts are tagged in Shopify.
- Rankings are a lagging check. The leading check is whether the page is indexed, the FAQ rich result is eligible, and internal links are crawlable.
